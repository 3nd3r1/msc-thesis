## CAD cascade

On reply edges link expansion on top of the proxy saves oracle calls on identity, affiliation, person and slur at every target, see step 4 in the [03 README](../../experiments/03_link_guided_search/README.md).
It is the first dataset where links help on top of the proxy, on Cora and Stack they made it worse.
Thread edges flip sign between targets and author edges stay close to the proxy.

## proxy+switch

Added `proxy+switch`, which picks between the proxy and links by Thompson sampling on which has hit more, as a first answer to RQ3, see step 4 in the [03 README](../../experiments/03_link_guided_search/README.md).
It needs no check before the search, it learns from the oracle calls the search makes anyway.
It is close to the proxy or better almost everywhere and costs up to 3.7 points on Stack.
On CAD thread edges it beats both orders it picks between.
