## Links do not save calls on SYNERGY

Walker finished, so all 10 reviews are in.

Links alone loses on 8.
Tables are in step 2 of `experiments/03_link_guided_search/README.md`.

The losses are as large as the wins, and the wins change with the target.
The tail check said citation reaches the hard positives on 8 reviews, but only 3 turn that into fewer calls.
This is the same picture as Stack.

The test that matters is proxy+links against the proxy.
On Cora and Stack links on top of the proxy were worse.
If SYNERGY shows the same, links do not help on any dataset so far.
