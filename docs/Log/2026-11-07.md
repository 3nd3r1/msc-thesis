## Proxy is done on every review

Walker finished and the missing calls on the other reviews were filled over three passes.
43 rows still have no verdict, none of them included, so the 0 that `proxy_scores` gives them cannot change calls to any recall target.
They are not API errors.
The 8B model starts reasoning, like "To determine whether the paper meets...", and runs out of the 8 tokens before saying True or False.
DeepInfra is not deterministic at temperature 0, so a retry sometimes answers, which is why each pass filled about half.
Cascade is rerun on the full proxy.

## CAD links carry something the embedding does not

CAD, the Contextual Abuse Dataset, is 23,417 Reddit entries labelled by annotators who saw the thread, and 28 to 35% of the abuse labels needed the previous post.
The reply, thread and author graphs overlap a matched cosine kNN by Jaccard 0.014 to 0.049, and the lift on edges outside the kNN stays near the full lift except on Slur.
Adding the share of positive graph neighbours to the embedding and its kNN neighbours raises out of fold AP on all 5 categories and all 3 graphs, by 0.003 to 0.036.
From `playground/cad_screen/results/links.txt`.
One fold split, no sd, and the neighbour labels are true labels, so this is an upper bound and the small gains may be noise.
