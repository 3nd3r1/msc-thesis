## Degree controls on SYNERGY

Read the `degree` and `blend/deg` columns in the SYNERGY `predicates.txt` files.
Degree alone, which reads no labels, does as well as links alone on most reviews, so much of what links do on SYNERGY is degree and not correlation.
Dividing by the degree fixes most of what `blend 1.0` lost, which fits the Nov 6 guess that counting negative neighbours punishes the well connected included papers.
Embeddings still win on every review, so this does not change the SYNERGY verdict.

## Looking for a second dataset like CAD

Screened datasets whose labels were given in context with the CAD screen, in `playground/context_screen`.
MELD, emotion per utterance in Friends dialogues, passes on reply and dialogue edges, with larger AP gains than CAD had.
Switchboard dialogue acts fail, since a reply is mostly a different act than the line before it, a question followed by an answer, so expanding from positives has nothing to follow.
The Reddit and Gab hate conversations fail too, hate sits in the text itself and does not cluster along replies.
Coarse Discourse ships no text, Conversations Gone Awry has one attack per conversation, RumourEval on HF has no reply tree and DEBAGREEMENT's download page is gone.

## MELD is in 03

`meld` has an edge to the previous line in the dialogue and `meld_dialogue` links every pair in a dialogue, both share `labels/meld`.
The claims in `MELD_PREDICATES` are a first draft and only matter if the proxy is run.
`predicates` runs on the desktop.

## The 8B works on MELD once told to judge the line alone

Checked the proxy on a balanced sample per emotion before any full run, in `playground/meld_proxy`.
Under plain LOTUS the 8B refuses a fifth to a third of the calls, asking for the scene, which is the same missing context that makes MELD interesting.
Asking the refused calls again with a prompt that says to judge the line alone gets a verdict on almost all of them, and the AUC then lands in the same range as CAD's.
So MELD can go to step 4, with the same two-prompt mix as CAD.
