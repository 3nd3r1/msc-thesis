## Why CAL is fine in production

The day is the cost of the bootstrap, which reruns about 2000 CAL orders against stored labels, not of the method.
A real query runs one CAL order, a few minutes of refits, next to thousands of oracle calls that take hours and cost money.
The thesis should report the order's own overhead next to the oracle time.
