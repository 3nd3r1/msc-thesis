## Links help on CAD reply edges

On reply edges links and embeddings beat embeddings alone on identity, affiliation and person at every target, see step 2 in the [03 README](../../experiments/03_link_guided_search/README.md).
It is the first dataset where links win the same predicates at every target.
The gain is small, at 90% about 5 to 8% of the calls embeddings need.
Thread edges flip sign between targets and author edges help reliably only on identity.
Whether any of it survives the proxy is what step 4 on CAD decides.
