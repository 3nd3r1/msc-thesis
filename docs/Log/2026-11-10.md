## The 8B refuses CAD

Under the plain LOTUS prompt the 8B gave no verdict on 51,024 of 117,085 CAD calls.
It refuses, with replies like "I cannot determine whether the post is abusive", also on posts the annotators did not mark.
`proxy_scores` gives a missing verdict 0, so cascade would send those rows last.
On the rows it did answer the AUC against the human labels is 0.62 to 0.85.
On 200 refused calls, adding "Always answer True or False" left 63 refused, and framing the prompt as content moderation left 11.
The refused calls are redone with the moderation prompt, so CAD's proxy scores mix two prompts.
The redo stopped partway and resumes from `labels/cad/proxy.jsonl`.
`results/cad/proxy.txt` is left uncommitted, since it is from the first pass and is rewritten when the redo finishes.
