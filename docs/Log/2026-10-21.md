# 2026-10-21

## The blend orders failed

Tried the blend orders on both edge sets and got nothing.
The Oct 16 null was the links and not the combiner.
Numbers are in step 2 of the [readme](../../experiments/03_link_guided_search/README.md).

## Sped up predicates

Searches run in a process pool now, the matvec is skipped for the orders that never read it, and BLAS is pinned to one thread per worker.
800 searches went from 221s to 66s.
Call counts are unchanged.

## The proxy wins on Stack too

Ran proxy and cascade on both Stack edge sets.
The 8B proxy beats every step 2 order on all 11 predicates at every target, and link expansion on top of it helps in 2 of the 44 cells on each edge set.
Numbers are in step 4 of the readme.

## A trained classifier cannot catch the proxy either

Added a ceiling step, logistic regression on the embeddings scored out of fold, which bounds anything that learns from verdicts.
It beats embeddings on all 18 predicates but loses to the proxy on 9 of 11 on Stack and 6 of 7 on Cora.
So trick 1 from [learning from verdicts](../Ideas/Learning%20from%20verdicts.md) needs the proxy score as a feature, not embeddings alone.
Numbers are in `results/*/ceiling.txt`.
