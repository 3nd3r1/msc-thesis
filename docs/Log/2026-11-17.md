## Big picture thoughts

The RQ3 screens cannot pass or fail.
The 56 rows are 29 label sets copied across edge sets, about 4 independent groups.
The gain has no seed noise since the proxy orders are deterministic but a bootstrap over threads dialogues and authors gives a median sd of about 2 points, up to 5 to 7 on the rare predicates.
With that noise the joint screen would pass about 6% of the time, so today's fail and the AP and refusal passes say nothing.

Every screen tracks the label source.
A plain human labels indicator has Spearman -0.672 against the gain, above every screen.
CAD and MELD were labelled by people who saw the thread or the scene, Cora and Stack by the 70B from the row text, so a row only oracle can not depend on the neighbours there.
Short positives like "Fine." for anger on MELD or "Easy there Hitler." for person on CAD show this.

`proxy+links` puts any neighbour of a positive above every other row whatever its p, so on Cora and Stack it measures this rule rather than links.
`proxy+diffuse` adds the residual with weight 1 to a score in 0 to 1, and at 0.1 to 0.3 it is about even with the proxy on CAD, so the 04 conclusion that one hop diffusion does not add is overstated.

The gain is set by two tail quantities, the hit rate of frontier rows the proxy ranks below its stopping depth against the proxy's precision where it stops.
Comparing them gets the sign of the gain right in 43 of 48 cells.
Both need labels, which is why label free statistics only separate datasets, and the RQ3 check should be online.

No paper found runs an LLM semantic filter with a recall target over given row links.
PROBE (arXiv 2611.01454) is closest, with residual diffusion over an embedding kNN and an F1 objective.
Pavlopoulos et al. 2020, Menini et al. 2021 and the CAD paper show that context changes abuse labels.

## 05 the oracle with the neighbour's text fails

Given the parent post on CAD or the line before on MELD, the 70B finds 2.1 points more of the human positives on average where 10 passes, see [05](../../experiments/05_context_oracle/README.md).
From the row alone it misses about a third of the CAD positives and about half of the MELD ones.
Reading every disagreement in `experiments/05_context_oracle/results/cases.txt`, most are not about the neighbour.
On CAD the annotators follow their own guidelines, "Bigot." under an abusive parent is identity abuse, "Citation needed." under an antisemitic post is counterspeech and "bitch" is a slur.
On MELD the emotion is mostly in the voice and face, like "That's terrible." for anger.
The neighbour fixes some, like "Add TheYellowRose to that list!" under a list of mods being sworn at.
DeepInfra served Llama 3.3 70B for 05 while 03 was labelled by 3.1 70B.

## What 05 means

With the oracle taken as the ground truth, as SUPG and the cascade papers do, the 70B disagreeing with the CAD annotators only says it is a worse labeller, and the 03 gains stand.
On CAD and MELD the oracle is effectively a human, which should be said in the thesis.
05 used the 70B as an instrument for why links help and it was a weak one.
CAD records per label whether the annotator needed the previous content, `annotation_Context`, all counterspeech, no slur and about a quarter to a third of the abuse labels.
SYNERGY is human labelled with the context outside the row but not in the neighbours, and links do not help there, so human labels alone do not explain the CAD and MELD gains.
That links help where the label sits in the neighbours is the premise, and the thesis adds when, by how much, that lift does not predict it, and that the switch keeps the gain at little cost elsewhere.

## Not ready to lock the design

03 compares my own orders against each other at a recall known in hindsight, which gives no number against a published method.
A databases thesis needs a head to head number, the same guarantee as LOTUS, BARGAIN, SUPG or PROBE at fewer oracle calls.
Binary predicates are not the problem, a semantic filter is yes or no in those papers too.
The gaps are the metric, cost at a guaranteed recall estimated from a sample, and the datasets and baselines, run under their own rules.
RQ3 is barely explored, for example an LLM picking which fields or links to follow.
The design lock planned for the end of November moves.
