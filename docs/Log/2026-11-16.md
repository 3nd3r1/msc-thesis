## Refusal rate as the RQ3 check

The refusal rate of the 8B on 300 random rows per predicate passes the rule fixed before the run, Spearman -0.634 against gain at 90% recall where -0.3 passes, from `playground/refusal_screen/results/refusal.txt`.
The pass comes from the split between datasets, Cora and Stack refuse nothing and CAD and MELD refuse 13 to 64%.
Within CAD it says nothing about the edge set, the three edge sets share the refusal rates and their gains differ in sign.
So it can tell whether links may help on a dataset, not which edges to use.

## proxy+diffuse fails on raw proxy scores

Added `proxy+diffuse`, the proxy plus the mean residual of the asked neighbours, one hop of the bias diffusion PROBE uses, over the links, a cosine kNN and both.
It is worse than the proxy on most cells of every dataset and graph, see step 4 in the [03 README](../../experiments/03_link_guided_search/README.md).
It loses as badly on the kNN as on the links, so this is likely the 8B's p near 1, where one negative pushes a row below every untouched one, rather than diffusion failing.
