## Ideas

Wrote some loose ideas as one framing in [Structure outside the row](../Ideas/Structure%20outside%20the%20row.md).
They are all the same question, what cheap information outside the row text tells a cascade where the positives are.
Three mechanisms: schema columns, entity metadata as a prior, and links between rows.
Prescreening products on their metadata is the cheapest to test since it needs no new review labels.
