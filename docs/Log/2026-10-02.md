# 2026-10-02

## Experiment 2

Wrote the replay and ran it three times, and it did not go well.
The sample rule decided the answer every run, so the experiment ended up measuring csv's sampling rule rather than the groupings.
With csv's floor of 100 rows the product groups are too small to sample at all.
Without the floor every small group samples one row, which agrees with itself, so the target stops meaning anything.
Also checked whether re-sampling all reviews of the sampled products would fix it, and it would not.

Rating is the only variant that clearly beats csv, and only on the two predicates the star rating partly defines.
Conclusion and numbers are in the [readme](../../experiments/02_cascade_replay/README.md).

Moved the shared code around on the way, lib holds only generic things now.

## Ideas

Wrote some loose ideas as one framing in [Structure outside the row](../Ideas/Structure%20outside%20the%20row.md).
They are all the same question, what cheap information outside the row text tells a cascade where the positives are.
Three mechanisms: schema columns, entity metadata as a prior, and links between rows.
Prescreening products on their metadata is the cheapest to test since it needs no new review labels.
