## Picked the SYNERGY reviews for the replay

`playground/synergy_screen/pick.py` keeps the active reviews with enough positives where citation reaches a positive.
Active is the same set as the reviews with eligibility criteria, so the proxy can be prompted on all of them.
The pick does not use lift, since lift does not predict calls (Oct 21).

## Citation reaches the hard positives on most reviews

The tail is the positives the embedding order finds between 80 and 95% recall, which is where links can save calls.
On 8 of the 10 reviews at least 45% of the tail positives have a citation edge to a positive found before them.
Numbers are in `playground/synergy_screen/results/tail.txt`.

This is an upper bound.
A link to an earlier positive does not mean the links order picks that neighbour next.
Most tails are 6 to 15 papers, so the small reviews are noisy.
Walker and Muthu are two of the three large tails and the weakest in the table.

On Stack the links led to rows the text found anyway.
Here a real share of the hard positives is reachable by citation, so the replay is worth building.

## SYNERGY lists some works more than once

Some review files list the same OpenAlex work more than once, the same paper found by pmid and by doi.
On Muthu and Leenaars many are listed once excluded and once included.
The copies gave the embeddings order free hits, and citations attached to one copy only.
`load_synergy` now keeps one row per work, included if any of its rows is.
The pick keeps the same 10 reviews and the tail check barely moved, so the entry above holds.
The screen in `playground/synergy_screen/results/screen.txt` is still from the duplicated pools.
