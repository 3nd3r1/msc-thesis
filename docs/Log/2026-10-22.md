## The author graph carries signal the embeddings do not

Compared the author edges against a cosine kNN graph at the same density.
The two share 201 edges out of a union of 9,465, a Jaccard of 0.02.
Author edges have a median cosine of 0.162 against 0.585 for the cosine edges, so they join rows the embedding puts far apart.
Dropping every author edge the cosine graph also contains leaves the lift within a few tenths of the full author lift on all 11 predicates, and the control stays at 1.0x.

This revises the Oct 21 entry, which read the author null as duplicate signal from the embeddings.
That held for postLinks and does not hold for author.

## Author failed on reach, and the reach came from the pool

The author graph reaches 35 to 56% of positives, against 100% for a cosine kNN, which gives every row a neighbour by construction.
The targets in 03 start at 50%, so expansion runs out before the first target on most predicates.
Positives with a positive neighbour stay under 40% everywhere, and that is what expansion actually spends.

The pool is the postLinks giant component, so it was selected for one graph and the author graph was tested on the leftovers.
Of all 42,179 questions, 29,373 are by an author with more than one.
A pool picked for authors would reach 100%, at the cost of relabelling.

## Every other Stack graph is dead

Screened each graph the dump can build against a matched cosine kNN, lift and reach averaged over the 10 real predicates, co- graphs capped at 20 posts per user.

| graph        | edges  | lift | ctrl | reach | jacc  |
| ------------ | ------ | ---- | ---- | ----- | ----- |
| postLinks    | 5,653  | 3.6x | 2.2x | 96.7% | 0.080 |
| author       | 5,665  | 2.8x | 1.0x | 42.3% | 0.021 |
| co-answerer  | 10,265 | 1.4x | 1.3x | 35.2% | 0.005 |
| co-commenter | 8,120  | 1.6x | 1.6x | 37.6% | 0.007 |
| co-favorite  | 2,087  | 0.5x | 0.7x | 3.0%  | 0.002 |
| shared-tag   | 12,509 | 2.1x | 1.0x | 38.1% | 0.040 |

The cosine kNN reference is 5.6x with a 1.7x control at 4,001 edges, and no graph in the table beats it.

ctrl is the lift on the we control, which has no reason to follow any graph, so only the gap above it is signal.
postLinks has a smaller gap than author despite the higher lift.
The three behavioural graphs have no gap at all, which is what kills them.
They were the plan from earlier today and cost one script to rule out.

Upvotes cannot be used at all, since the dump strips UserId on every one of them.
Favorites are the only vote type that keeps a user.

## What the screen says to look for

Cosine kNN is a strong reference on Stack and Cora because the oracle decides from the row text, so the label is a function of what the embedding reads.
A graph only has room where the label is not.
So the label source is what has to change.

## None of the research questions are answered

RQ1 has lift numbers, but the Oct 21 union test showed the lift is partly an artifact of the oracle's label set, and the predicate kinds predicted none of the step 2 winners.
RQ2 is negative under one oracle that reads only the row text, which makes the text order near optimal by construction, so the null is a result about the setup.
RQ3 is untouched, with lift eliminated as one candidate check.

## SYNERGY graphs are competitive with the embedding, not better

Screened 29 of the 46 SYNERGY review files, citations and bibliographic coupling against a cosine kNN at matched edge count, on the rows that have an abstract.

| positives | reviews | a graph wins | win mean | loss mean |
| --------- | ------- | ------------ | -------- | --------- |
| any       | 29      | 12           | +2.1x    | -1.8x     |
| 30 plus   | 14      | 7            | +1.3x    | -0.8x     |
| 50 plus   | 9       | 6            | +0.8x    | -0.4x     |
| 80 plus   | 5       | 4            | +1.0x    | -1.1x     |

Per review numbers are in `playground/synergy_screen/results/screen_partial.txt`.

Inclusion rates run 0.9 to 26%, so most reviews hold 12 to 30 positives and every large entry in the table is noise.
The -12.9x on ACEInhibitors is 26 positives and the +13.4x on van der Waal is 22 positives against a cosine lift of 0.0x.
Requiring more positives raises the win rate and shrinks the margins to under 1x either way, and 6 of 9 is not distinguishable from chance.

Reach is what changed from Stack, 70 to 100% on almost every review against 42% for the author graph, with Jaccards under 0.2.
So these graphs are neither duplicates of the embedding nor too sparse to use, and they still do not beat it.
Direct citations are empty or near empty on several reviews, 6 edges on Menon_2022, so coupling is the denser of the two.

The control here is the lift with the labels permuted, which is weaker than a real control predicate.
