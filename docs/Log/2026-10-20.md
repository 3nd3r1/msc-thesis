## Step 2 tested one link rule, not link ordering

links+embeddings adds a neighbour count to a cosine, so a confirmed neighbour at 1 outranks any similarity at about 0.2 and links decide the order.
It also throws away every negative the oracle returns, and at a control lift of 2.2x most neighbours of a positive are negatives.
Added two orders, links+- for positive minus negative neighbours, and blend a for a * (positive - negative neighbours) + mean cosine.
Nothing run yet, so the null result from Oct 16 stands for now.

## predicates is slow

One embeddings search over 4,912 rows takes 6.3s, and the cost is the similarity matvec that fires once per positive found.
Precomputing the gram matrix takes 0.32s and would remove it, but its values do not match the per call matvec bitwise, so the committed numbers would not reproduce.
Left it alone.
