# 2026-11-13

## Links help on MELD on top of the proxy

Ran `cascade` on `meld` and `meld_dialogue` on the desktop.
From 80% recall link expansion on top of the proxy needs fewer calls than the proxy on every emotion except surprise, on both edge sets, see step 4 in the [03 README](../../experiments/03_link_guided_search/README.md).
Surprise is worse at every target, most on dialogue edges.
At 50% links are worse on most emotions on dialogue edges.
It is the second dataset after CAD where links help on top of the proxy.
Every order is still weak on MELD, links or not, reaching 90% recall takes about half the lines or more.
These use the default decay, so `proxy+switch` on MELD is rerun if the CAD sweep picks another.

## Theories, untested

- On dense edges, MELD dialogue and CAD thread, links lose to the proxy early and win late, since expanding from positives only beats the proxy once its easy rows are used up.
- On those dense edges `proxy+switch` beats both orders it picks between, while on sparse reply edges it follows links and pays for surprise.
- The cheap signals from the MELD proxy sample also order the step 4 gains roughly, surprise has the fewest refusals and the highest proxy AUC and gains nothing, anger is the outlier. Six emotions, and CAD did not follow the refusals in step 2.

## Error correlation along edges does not explain where links help

Over every predicate of the datasets with a proxy, neither the label nor the proxy residual correlation along the edges tracks what links add on top of the proxy at 90% recall, Spearman +0.10 and -0.12.
From `playground/residual_screen/results/residual.txt`.
So the Oct 21 finding that lift does not predict calls holds for the residual too, and a cheap check for RQ3 has to come from somewhere else.
