## Proxy

Ran the 8B on every row and predicate, 18,956 calls.
It beats every structural order on all seven predicates, needing 3.6 to 13.5% of the rows.

Its own verdicts are bad, a third to a tenth of the oracle's yes rate, biology worst at 0.4% against 4.2%.
So it ranks well and decides badly, which is why a cascade uses the score and not the verdict.

## Links on top of the proxy

Nothing worked.

proxy+links, preferring the unasked neighbours of a confirmed positive, is worse on six of seven and tied on the last.
proxy+neighbours, adding the mean proxy score of a row's neighbours, is worse on all seven.
Sweeping the weight on that gave per predicate wins, but leave one out across predicates picks weight 0 for every predicate on the clean pool.
The control gained the most in the sweep, which is the tell.

Deleted the smoothing code and its two result files, so weights and holdout cannot be regenerated without writing them again.

Four rules have failed now and all for the same reason.
The proxy's ranking is better than anything the graph adds, so moving rows away from it costs more than it gains.

## Where that leaves it

Step 2's win on markov and language is the no proxy regime only.
