## Which comparison grounds the thesis

Had an agent go through the baseline papers and the repo for the fairest comparison against published methods.
BARGAIN stays the main baseline, since it is the newest method with a finite sample recall guarantee and any score frozen before its sample plugs in without changing the method.
The budget is a user input in BARGAIN's query, the paper uses 400 on datasets of very different sizes and sweeps it itself, so setting it per dataset is fair while changing its internals or picking it after the results is not.
At 400 calls uniform BARGAIN returns every row on our rare predicates, as it does on Onto in its own paper.
Certifying 90% recall at δ 0.1 needs at least about 22 sampled positives, so no order can help where the rate times the budget is small.
PROBE is the secondary comparison, its kNN graph swapped for the links with nothing else changed.
CSV and CascadeFilter go to related work, their accuracy targets are met by the negatives on rare predicates.
Rebuilding Court and Wiki with their links is dropped, they are labelled from the row text like Cora, Stack and SYNERGY where links never helped.
The link claim has to be made on CAD and MELD, the only datasets where the label sits in the neighbours.

## links.py in bargain_screen

`playground/bargain_screen/links.py` runs plain BARGAIN against BARGAIN on scores frozen after a first phase of search on the proxy alone, a cosine kNN, links rewired with the same degrees, and the real links, at the same total budget.
The budget grid, the phase 1 share and the score rule are fixed in the script before the run.
The headline is the cost of a verified set, the oracle calls over both phases plus checking every returned row, so the budget counts in the cost and is not a knob for one side.

## Four agents on what to do next

- Skeptic: the link claim as framed is not a headline. The 03 step 4 gain has no kNN or rewired control, so it is not yet attributed to links. Suggests a boundary study, budgeted verified search plus BARGAIN, with a fallback on oracle quality against human labels. Also says to book Lu by date, check PreThesis and the supervision agreement are filed, and book the seminar slot.
- Links in the certification: stratified, cluster, control variate and importance sampling designs are valid but can only move BARGAIN toward the hindsight prefix of a fixed order, a few points at most.
- Certification and query formulation agents both landed on a TAR stopping rule, Cormack and Grossman's target method and QBCB (Lewis, Yang, Frieder, CIKM 2021, arXiv 2108.12746). A uniform sample until k positives, hidden, then screen in any order and stop when enough hidden positives are reached. It gives a finite sample recall guarantee with a verified result and stays valid for an adaptive order like the 03 link order.
- In their offline checks proxy only with the rule roughly ties plain BARGAIN, and links on top cut the cost by about 2 to 12 points of rows on CAD reply edges and MELD, worse on surprise, with kNN close to links on CAD identity and affiliation. The same size as 03, now under a guarantee. k was not fixed before those runs. Scripts and output in `playground/tar_screen`.
- Better score agent, a learned score on proxy, embedding and link features certified by BARGAIN, was stopped before it reported.
