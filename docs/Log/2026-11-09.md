## Search is faster with the same results

A search step now rescores only the rows it changed, instead of every row.
Old and new give the same calls on Cora, Stack, SYNERGY Nelson and `cad_thread`.
On CAD the link and fixed orders went from about 9 s to 0.2 s per search, and the embedding orders from up to 23 s to about 5 s.
The embedding orders are still bound by one matvec per positive, which can't go without changing the results.

## Runs move to the desktop

The laptop was too slow for CAD and lagged while running.
The desktop is an i5-11600K, about 2 to 3 times the laptop.
The setup is WSL2 Ubuntu with Nix for `shell.nix`, reached from the laptop over Tailscale SSH.
WSL2 needed virtualisation turned on in the BIOS first.
The repo is cloned over HTTPS since the desktop never pushes.
`data/` goes over with rsync, results come back with rsync, and git stays on the laptop.
