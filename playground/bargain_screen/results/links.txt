BARGAIN_R uniform, recall target 0.9, delta 0.1, mean of 50 runs, pool with junk and repeated texts dropped

plain: BARGAIN on the 8B p(yes) with the full budget
proxy, knn, rewired, links: 25% of the budget on search first, then BARGAIN with the rest on the frozen scores
search asks the highest unasked row, a neighbour of a found positive goes above every untouched row
frozen scores: found positives, then neighbours of positives by p, then the rest by p, then found negatives
knn is the cosine kNN with k matched to the mean link degree, rewired keeps every row's link degree with random ends
met: share of runs with recall at the target, returned: share of rows returned
cost: distinct oracle calls over both phases plus the returned rows not yet asked, as a share of the rows
